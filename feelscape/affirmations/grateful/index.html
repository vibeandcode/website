How to use these

One line. Slowly.

Read one, then name three specific things: not "my family" but "the way my sister called back last night." Gratitude affirmations work when they are followed by detail. Specific is the whole trick.

FAQ

Questions about feeling grateful

Do gratitude affirmations actually work?
Gratitude practice has some of the strongest evidence in positive psychology, with studies linking it to better mood and sleep. Affirmations are simply a portable way to prompt it. The key is specificity, which is why FeelScape writes them from your own logged context.
How often should I practise gratitude?
A few times a week is enough for most people; daily can become rote. Logging "grateful" in FeelScape when you genuinely feel it beats forcing it on a schedule.
