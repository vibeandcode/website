FAQ

About this library

How do I choose the right affirmation?
Start with the mood, not the goal. An affirmation that names how you feel right now lands far better than one about who you want to be. Pick your mood above, read the ten lines slowly, and keep the one that made you exhale.
What makes these different from a quote list?
Every line was written for one specific feeling, in first person, in plain language. There is no "You are amazing!" here. This is the same principle the FeelScape app uses, except the app goes further and writes them for your exact mood, your notes and, with FeelAI Memory, your life.
Are affirmations backed by research?
Self-affirmation research (Steele, 1988; Cohen and Sherman, 2014) shows that reflecting on personally relevant values reduces defensiveness and stress responses. The key word is personally relevant, which is why generic affirmations underperform and personalized ones do better.
Can I get affirmations in my language?
The site is in English. The FeelScape app writes affirmations in 37 languages, chosen in Settings.
