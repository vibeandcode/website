How it learns

What feeds the memory, and what comes back.

Three questions to start. After that it learns from your notes, your replies and a review of your last two weeks every few days. You approve what it keeps.

What feeds it

1

Three questions

What you value, what you are working on, what wears you down. Pick from suggestions or write your own.

2

Your notes

One line with a mood, like “presentation tomorrow, barely slept”. FeelAI reads it and remembers it.

3

Your replies

Answer a check-in and FeelAI extracts what helped, what triggered you, how a goal is going.

4

Pattern review

Every few days it re-reads your last two weeks and proposes up to three things it noticed. You accept or reject each.

Your rights

We charge for attention. Not for the relationship.

Memory is yours. These rules are in FeelAI’s constitution and enforced in code.

  • Visible

    “This is how FeelAI sees you.” Every field, in plain language, under Settings → My Memory.

  • Editable

    Add, correct or remove any item. Suggestions from pattern review wait for your yes or no.

  • Deletable

    Remove one item or clear everything. Deletions are permanent and immediate.

  • Never hostage

    Cancel and your memory stays. It is never degraded or withheld to pull you back.

  • Never invented

    Inference is allowed; fabrication is not. If it has nothing specific, it stays warm and general.

  • Never shared

    Nothing traceable to you crosses to another user, to training, or to internal tooling.

FAQ

Premium questions

How does the free trial work?
Start Premium in the app and the first 3 days are free. Cancel before the trial ends and you are not charged. After that it renews at $4.99/month or $39.99/year depending on the plan you picked.
Why is memory the paid part?
Because it is the part that costs us attention: a stronger model, a pattern review every few days, a timed check-in, a monthly letter drawn from everything you have shared. Free FeelScape is the same companion with the same voice, writing from your mood alone.
What happens to my memory if I stop paying?
It stays. FeelScape’s constitution forbids removing or degrading memory on downgrade, and forbids implying the relationship ends. Come back and FeelAI picks up where it left off.
Can I cancel any time?
Yes, from your App Store or Google Play subscription settings. You keep Premium until the end of the paid period.
Is Premium worth it if I only check in a few times a month?
That is the case it was designed for. The goal written into FeelAI’s constitution is that if you open the app twice a month and feel understood both times, it is working. Memory is what makes the second time feel like the twentieth.
