Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how FeelScape (“the App”), made by vibe&code, collects, uses and protects your information. Your privacy matters to us, and we are committed to protecting your personal data.

2. Information we collect

Information you provide

  • Mood selections and optional notes used to generate personalized affirmations
  • Memory profile entries (values, goals, stressors, coping strategies, traits) if you use FeelAI Memory
  • Replies to check-ins
  • Preferences and settings, saved affirmations and favourites
  • Account details (email, or an Apple/Google sign-in identifier)

Automatically collected information

  • Device type and operating system
  • App usage statistics and crash reports
  • General location (country/region) for service optimization

3. How we use your information

  • Generate personalized affirmations, check-ins and letters based on what you log
  • Improve and optimize the App experience
  • Provide customer support
  • Analyze usage patterns to enhance features

4. AI processing

Your inputs are processed by AI systems to generate content. This processing may occur on our servers or through third-party AI service providers under contract. We do not use your personal inputs to train AI models without your explicit consent. Nothing traceable to you is shared with other users.

5. Data storage and security

We implement appropriate security measures to protect your data. Your data is stored on secure cloud servers so it syncs across your devices.

6. Data sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We may share data with service providers who assist in operating the App (hosting, AI processing, analytics, payments) and with legal authorities when required by law.

7. Your rights

  • Access your personal data, including everything FeelAI remembers about you
  • Correct or delete any memory entry, or clear it entirely
  • Request deletion of your account and data
  • Opt out of analytics collection
  • Export your saved data

8. Children’s privacy

The App is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes through the App or via email.

10. Contact us

Questions about this policy or your data: vibeandcode@gmail.com.
