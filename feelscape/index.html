What makes FeelScape different

It remembers you. So it gets better every month.

Every other affirmation app forgets you the moment you close it. FeelAI keeps a memory you can read: what you value, what you are working on, what wears you down, what has helped. Then it writes from that, and tells you why under every line.

  • It learns from what you tell itThree questions to start. After that, every note you add and every check-in you answer teaches it something. Nothing is inferred behind your back; it proposes what it noticed and you approve it.
  • It shows its workWhen memory shapes an affirmation, a short line underneath says what shaped it. If nothing specific applied, the line stays empty. You always know the difference between being known and being flattered.
  • It is yoursRead everything it remembers, in plain language. Edit or delete any of it. It is never held hostage: cancel, and your memory stays.

FAQ

Questions, answered

What is FeelScape?
FeelScape is a mood tracker that writes affirmations for how you feel. You tap one or more of 50 moods, optionally add a note, and FeelAI generates 3 affirmations for that exact state. It is free on iOS and Android.
How is it different from other affirmation apps?
Most affirmation apps show everyone the same quotes from a library. FeelScape generates each affirmation for your logged mood and note, and Premium members get FeelAI Memory, which remembers their values, goals and stressors so affirmations reference their real life. There are also no streaks: guilt is not a feature.
Is FeelScape free?
Yes. Mood logging, 3 affirmations per check-in, reminders, the widget and sharing are free. Premium ($4.99/month or $39.99/year, 3-day free trial) adds FeelAI Memory, 10 memory-aware affirmations per check-in, proactive check-ins timed to your dips, a monthly letter drawn from your memory, unlimited check-ins and custom moods.
What is FeelAI Memory?
A profile FeelAI keeps about you: values, goals, stressors, traits and coping strategies you give it, plus what has helped, known patterns and known triggers it learns from your notes, your check-in replies and a review of your last two weeks every few days. It shapes every affirmation, and a line under each one says what shaped it. You can read everything it remembers, edit or delete any item, and it is never used to pressure you into anything.
Is my mood data private?
Your mood data is stored securely, never sold, and never shared with third parties for advertising. AI processing happens on our servers or with AI providers under contract; your inputs are not used to train models without your explicit consent. You can export or delete your data any time.
Is FeelScape a mental health treatment?
No. FeelScape is a wellness companion, not a medical device or a substitute for therapy. If you are struggling, please talk to a professional. If you are in crisis, contact your local emergency number or a crisis line.
Which languages does it support?
37, including English, Spanish, French, German, Italian, Portuguese, Japanese, Korean, Chinese, Hindi and Arabic. Both the app and the affirmations FeelAI writes follow your chosen language.
