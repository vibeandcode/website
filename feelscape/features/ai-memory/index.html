Your rights over it

The memory belongs to you, not to us.

These are written into FeelAI’s constitution, the document that governs what it may and may not do. The code enforces them; the prompts do not get a vote.

  • Visible

    “This is how FeelAI sees you.” Every field, in plain language, under Settings → My Memory.

  • Editable

    Add, correct or remove any item. Suggestions from pattern review wait for your yes or no.

  • Deletable

    Remove one item or clear everything. Deletions are permanent and immediate.

  • Never hostage

    Cancel and your memory stays. It is never degraded or withheld to pull you back.

  • Never invented

    Inference is allowed; fabrication is not. If it has nothing specific, it stays warm and general.

  • Never shared

    Nothing traceable to you crosses to another user, to training, or to internal tooling.

What it will never do

No streaks. No guilt. No upsell when you are low.

  • —It never mentions streaks, scores or how often you log.
  • —It never speaks outside 9 am to 8 pm, and silence is always an option for it.

  • —It never uses distress as a moment to sell. Distress is when it is least commercial.
  • —It never uses medical or diagnostic language, and never claims to be a therapist.

FAQ

Memory questions

What exactly does FeelAI remember?
Eight things: your values, goals, known stressors, traits, coping strategies, what has helped, known patterns and known triggers. The first five come from you; the last three are learned from your notes, your check-in replies and a review of your last two weeks every few days. All of it is visible under Settings → My Memory.
Does it make things up about me?
No. Every specific it writes must trace to something you recorded, and the “written for you” line under each affirmation tells you what that was. If nothing specific applies, it leaves the line empty rather than inventing one.
Can I correct or delete what it knows?
Yes. Add, edit or remove any item. Pattern-review suggestions wait for your approval. There is also a Clear All Memory option; it is permanent.
What happens to my memory if I cancel?
It stays. FeelScape charges for attention, not for the relationship: memory is never removed or degraded on downgrade, and if you come back FeelAI picks up where it left off.
How is this different from a journaling app with AI?
A journal stores what you wrote. Memory turns it into a profile that shapes what you hear next: the affirmations, the timing of check-ins, the monthly letter. Day 200 sounds different from day 1 because it knows more.
Is it safe?
A deterministic safety layer sits on every message. No medical or diagnostic language, no claim to be a therapist, and risk signals route straight to crisis resources without passing through the model. If the safety layer is unavailable, FeelAI stays silent.
