How can we help?

Email vibeandcode@gmail.com, or fill in the form and it will open a pre-filled message in your email app. We usually reply within 24 to 48 hours.

Manage or cancel a subscription

Subscriptions are handled by Apple or Google. iPhone: Settings → your name → Subscriptions. Android: Play Store → profile → Payments & subscriptions.

Restore a purchase

In the app: Settings → Subscription → Restore Purchases. Make sure you are signed in with the same account you used to buy.

Export or delete your data

Settings → Account. Deletion removes moods, notes, memory and affirmations permanently.

Something not working

Tell us your device, OS version and what you expected to happen. Screenshots help.
